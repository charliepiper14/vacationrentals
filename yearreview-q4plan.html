Wildwood and Ocean City, the Growth Story

Wildwood tripled its bookings, 3,006 against 945, while cutting cost per booking to $4.39 from $6.45. Ocean City added 72% more bookings at $5.62 against $7.43. Both were under-invested a year ago and both scaled profitably, well inside their $7.00 and $10.00 targets across the full twelve months.

Long Beach Island, the Efficiency Story

The anchor is the opposite shape and just as deliberate. A year ago it ran a sprawl of brand, exact, DSA and remarketing campaigns, with brand exact alone booking over 11,000. This year it is a lean Generic plus PMax at $0.83, half the prior $1.74. Spend came down two-thirds because the brand traffic we stopped paying for is now captured by your strong organic for free. What remains is incremental non-brand, and it is the most efficient engine in the portfolio.

The Berger Milestone on Ocean City

Across the season we closed the Ocean City impression-share gap to Berger from 14.55 points to as tight as 1.33 mid-August, on scaling and rank alone, before the conquest campaign was even deployed. That gap, and the conquest held in reserve, is a live advantage carried into 2027.

Strategic Plan - Q4 2026 and Into 2027

Q4 Is Protect and Prepare: Hold Efficiency Through the Dead Season, Load the Spring

Q4 is the quiet quarter, so the plan is to protect efficiency now and be ready to move the moment demand returns after Christmas. Hold the dormant floor on the $7 shutoff through October and November, keep the audience pool warm, and have the December reactivation and the 2027 structure built and waiting so we open the new season ahead of the market rather than catching up.

Through Q4, October to December
  • Hold all three at the dormant floor, flight off, automated scripts easing spend if any lead cost creeps over the $7 shutoff. PMax is the line to keep trimmed in the thin auction.
  • Keep the summer pool live as lookalike and reactivation audiences, it carries close to two years and feeds the winter ramp.
  • Prepare the December reactivation push to catch the post-Christmas surge early, when your booking curve turns sharply back up.

The Plan

  • Timing. An 8-week summer flight across the in-season window when the most unique families cycle through the rentals, plus a lighter reactivation burst from mid-December to re-warm the pool ahead of the post-Christmas surge
  • Budget. Hold about $7,200 a week, roughly $57,600 across the flight, funded from reallocated Google underspend rather than the booking budget, heavier on Wildwood and Ocean City where awareness is the constraint. Flexes to a ceiling, if budget tightens we cut frequency not weeks
  • Markets and geofencing. One StackAdapt account, a campaign per market, bridges fenced as arrival capture and boardwalks, beaches and main draws fenced for dwell-time capture, CTV by household and zip with the geofences on the mobile line
  • Creative. 30s and 15s spots for CTV at broadcast quality, 6s cuts for retargeting still to be delivered, three concepts carried across all formats, Home Away from Home, Skip Booking Fees, Location and Attractions
  • Measurement. Primary on reach and unique households, pixel on all three sites capturing visitors into the pool, view-through and click-through both on, kept entirely separate from the Google booking CPA line
The Piece That Joins It to Google

The point of the pool is to spend it. For 2027 the CTV and site audiences export into Google as Customer Match and audience signals, so the warm Shore audience is retargeted through Demand Gen and fed into Search and PMax during the booking window. The sequence is simple, the summer flight builds the pool, it is held as a first-party asset through the autumn at no cost, the December burst re-warms it, and Google retargets it hard through the January to July window at the lowest cost per booking in the plan.
